About this project

Hi, I'm Phoebie.

I explore AI tools, share what I learn, and turn the clearest lessons into short, useful explanations.

What I make

I publish daily YouTube Shorts about the tools I am exploring. On this site, I organize the useful details that do not fit into a short video: what a tool is, how I tested it, where it helps, and where it falls short.

How I choose tools

I try the tools before I recommend them. Reviews are based on a real task, and recommendations should explain the test and its limits instead of promising results.

How the site makes money

Some links may be affiliate links. If an adult reader buys through one of those links, this site may earn a commission at no extra cost to the reader. Affiliate programs do not decide the review verdict.

Parent-managed

A parent reviews publishing, business accounts, affiliate relationships, and public contact details for this project.
