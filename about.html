About us

Hi, we're the Wang family.

Phoebie is 10 and builds apps and puzzles. Her mom, Sabrina Zhang, writes novels and makes printables, songs and videos. This site is where it all lives.

What we make

  • Free practice. A fresh set of math and Chinese every day, plus visual lessons, mental-math tricks and games. All free, with no sign-up.
  • Printables. Worksheets, calligraphy posters and coloring pages on Etsy, from $1.99.
  • Books. Sabrina writes novels under the name Sabrina Zhang. See Books & Songs.
  • Songs and videos. Original songs in Chinese and English, each with a music video.
  • Apps. Phoebie built Bird Pal Buddies, an app that helps pet birds learn to talk.

Who is behind it

Sabrina Zhang runs the site, the shop and the publishing. Phoebie is 10, comes up with the games and builds the apps with her mom's help. We are a family project, not a company, and we are not a school or a tutoring service.

How we handle your family's data

The lessons run in your browser and nothing you type is sent to us. There are no accounts, no ads and no trackers. Read the privacy notice for the details.

How the site pays for itself

Free practice stays free. We earn money from the printables sold on Etsy and from our books. A few unlisted pages contain affiliate links; see the affiliate disclosure.

Contact

Message us through our Etsy shop. We read every message.
