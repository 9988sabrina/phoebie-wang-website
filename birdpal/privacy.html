Bird Pal Buddies Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Bird Pal Buddies helps families practice words with a pet bird. It was made by Phoebie, a young app maker, with help from her family.

We don't collect your data

The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We don't collect, store or share any personal information.

Your recordings stay on your device

When you record a word, the recording is saved only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. It is never uploaded. Deleting a word or the app removes its recordings.

Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when you tap "Hold to record my voice". It records only while you hold the button.

Children

Bird Pal Buddies is designed to be used by children with a grown-up. Because it collects no information, there is nothing to share with anyone.
