Home / Learning Lab / Chickens & Rabbits

Grades 4–6 · Logic puzzle · English & 中文

10 heads, 28 legs. How many chickens and rabbits?

Leo asks, Lily answers. Watch two kids talk through a classic puzzle in about two minutes, then try the challenge before the timer runs out.

How to watch

  • 1Tap Start and turn your sound on. Each line is voiced, and the animals act out the math.
  • 2Tap 中 / EN any time to switch language. The voices, captions and problem all switch together.
  • 3Use ◀ and ▶ to go back over a step, or pause and ask your child what comes next.
The trick to rememberPretend they're all chickens. Missing legs ÷ 2 = rabbits.Every chicken you swap for a rabbit keeps the heads the same and adds 2 legs.
Challenge answer (no peeking!)

20 heads, 56 legs: all chickens would be 40 legs, so 16 are missing. 16 ÷ 2 = 8 rabbits, and 20 − 8 = 12 chickens. Check: 12×2 + 8×4 = 24 + 32 = 56.
