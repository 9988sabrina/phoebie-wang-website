Multiply by 5 in a flash

Half the number. Put a 0 on the end. Done. Two fives always make a ten, and that is the whole secret. There is a second trick below for times 25. 乘 5 的秘密：先取一半，再在末尾加 0。

See why it worksBigger numbers

🖐️Fives snap into tens

Pick how many fives you have. Every two fives click together to make one ten, so you only need to count the tens.

Example: 8 × 5 → eight fives make 4 tens, so 8 × 5 = 40. Half of 8 is 4, then a 0.

8 × 5 = 40

8 fives snap into 4 tens. Half of 8 is 4, then a 0: 40.

Even number?Half it, then put a 0 on the end. 14 × 5 → half is 7 → 70.
Odd number?Half the number just below it, then put a 5 on the end. 15 × 5 → half of 14 is 7 → 75.

⚡Bigger numbers, and times 25

The same trick works for any number. The last two cards are times 25: take a quarter (half, then half again) and put two zeros on the end. Type each answer and tap Check.

The tricks: 46 × 5 → half of 46 is 23 → 230. 16 × 25 → a quarter of 16 is 4 → 400. Five is half of ten, and 25 is a quarter of 100. 乘 5 = 取一半再加一个 0；乘 25 = 取四分之一再加两个 0。
