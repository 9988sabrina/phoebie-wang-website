Multiply by 9 with your fingers

Hold up both hands. Fold one finger. Read the answer. That is the whole trick for 1 to 10, and there is a second trick below for bigger numbers. 手指算九的乘法：弯下一根手指，左边是十位，右边是个位。

Try it nowBigger numbers

✋Fold a finger

Number your fingers 1 to 10 from left to right. To work out a number times 9, fold that finger down. The fingers on the left of the fold are the tens, the fingers on the right are the ones.

Example: 4 × 9 → fold finger 4. That leaves 3 fingers on the left and 6 on the right, so 4 × 9 = 36.

  • folded
  • 7 ones

3 × 9 = 27

Fold finger 3. 2 fingers on the left, 7 on the right, so 3 × 9 = 27.

🔟Bigger numbers: times 10, take one away

Fingers run out at 10. For any other number, multiply by 10 first (just add a zero), then subtract the number once. Type each answer and tap Check.

The trick: 23 × 9 → 23 × 10 = 230, then 230 − 23 = 207. Nine is just ten with one taken away. 乘 9 等于乘 10 再减去自己一次。
