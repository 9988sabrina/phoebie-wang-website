How to play · 玩法

Three rules

  1. Tap a car and it drives the way its arrow points. If another car is in the way it just wiggles.
  2. Parked cars fill up. Passengers join the car with the same colour and shape. A full car drives away.
  3. Keep a bay free. If all five bays hold cars that nobody can board, the lot is jammed. Undo and think ahead!

Stars: 3 for no hints and no undos, 2 for one or two, 1 otherwise. Bumping into a car is free, so explore!

Math bonus: after each level the driver asks a quick mental-math question: multiply by 11, or double-and-half (12 × 5 = 6 × 10). Get it right for a math star; get it wrong and you learn the trick. Turn it off with the Math button.
