Privacy Notice

Short and plain. Last updated November 5, 2026.

What this site does with your data

  • There are no accounts, public profiles, comments, chat, ads, advertising pixels or third-party analytics.
  • Lesson and practice answers are checked in your browser and are not sent to us.
  • Some games save progress (score, level, best time, today's puzzle) in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device. The "Clear local progress" button in the footer removes it.

Weekly email

A weekly practice-page email is being set up and is not switched on yet. When it is, we will ask only for an adult's email address, use it only to send that email, and put an unsubscribe link in every message.

Bird Pal Buddies and the microphone

The Bird Pal app asks to use your microphone only after you tap its mic button. See the Bird Pal privacy page for details.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it can see technical information such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time, in its server and security logs.

Other sites we link to

Etsy, Amazon and YouTube handle your payment and account information under their own notices. Children should not enter purchase information. If you buy something, an adult should do it.

Children

The site has no feature that asks a child for personal information. Adults should supervise use. Please do not send us a child's school, address, birthday, location, photo or recording.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: message us through our Etsy shop.
