Privacy Notice — Draft

A plain-language description of the current preview. It is not a claim of complete legal compliance.

Parent and legal review required before launch. The operator identity, effective date, contact address, final host, and hosted checkout data flow must be confirmed.

Current preview behavior

  • No child account, public profile, chat, comment, photo upload, audio upload, email signup, advertising pixel, or third-party analytics is enabled.
  • Lesson answers are checked in the browser and are not sent to this website.
  • The Play game can save score, level, and best time in browser local storage under phoebieplays-state-v2.
  • The “Clear local progress” control removes the site's local-storage entries from that browser.

Hosting and technical records

Even without a form, a web host can receive technical information such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, and time of access in server or security logs. The current live site is hosted on GitHub Pages; production hosting is planned to change before commercial launch. The final host's retention and privacy terms must be reviewed.

External checkout

No checkout is active in this preview. If a parent later enables the Payhip link, adult buyers will leave this site. Payhip and the connected payment processor will handle information needed for payment, receipts, tax, fraud prevention, and digital delivery under their own notices. Children should not enter purchase information.

Children's privacy

The site is designed without features that ask children to provide personal information. Adults should supervise use. Do not submit a child's school, address, birthday, location, schedule, photo, recording, or private contact details.

Contact and changes
