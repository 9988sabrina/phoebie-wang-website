Double & half multiplication

Halve one factor, double the other, and the product stays the same. That is the whole trick behind ×25 and ×50.

Who it is for

Learners around U.S. grades 4–5 who already know basic multiplication and want a mental-math strategy for ×25 and ×50. A parent or teacher should decide if it fits.

What students practice

  • Halving one even factor while doubling the other.
  • Explaining why the product stays unchanged.
  • Turning ×25 or ×50 into multiplication by 100.
  • Using the strategy in short word problems.
  • Finding and explaining errors in worked examples.
