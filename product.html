Double & Half Multiplication Practice Pack

A focused printable pack for practicing one useful multiplication strategy with increasing independence.

This product is not for sale yet. Content, price, license, support contact, and delivery must be approved by a parent before launch.

Who it is for

Designed for learners working around U.S. grades 4-5 who already understand basic multiplication and want a mental-math strategy for ×25 and ×50. A parent, homeschool educator, or permitted teacher should choose whether it fits a specific learner.

What students practice

  • Halving one even factor while doubling the other.
  • Explaining why the product stays unchanged.
  • Turning ×25 or ×50 into multiplication by 100.
  • Applying the strategy in short word problems.
  • Finding and explaining errors in worked examples.

What the 10 pages contain

  1. Strategy overview and the 48 × 25 visual model.
  2. Worked examples and a “why it works” model.
  3. Guided ×25 practice.
  4. Independent ×25 practice.
  5. Guided and independent ×50 practice.
  6. Mixed strategy choice.
  7. Application problems.
  8. Error analysis and explanation prompts.
  9. Answers for pages 3-5.
  10. Answers and teaching notes for pages 6-8.

No repeated cover pages or filler pages are included.

Preview and printing
