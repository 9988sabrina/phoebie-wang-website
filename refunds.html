Digital Products & Refunds

What to know before you buy a printable from our Etsy shop. Last updated November 5, 2026.

Where we sell

Our printables are sold on our Etsy shop. Etsy handles payment, receipts and the instant download. This website has no checkout.

What you get

Each item is a digital file (usually a PDF) for printing at home. Nothing is shipped. Please read the listing and look at the free sample page before you buy.

If something is wrong

If a file is missing, damaged, will not open, or has a math mistake, message us through Etsy with your order details and we will fix or replace it, usually within 2 to 3 business days. Never send full card details.

Refunds

Because digital files can be downloaded right away, we do not offer change-of-mind refunds. We will help with duplicate charges and with files that are broken or not as described. Etsy's own purchase protection and your local consumer rights still apply.
