Thanksgiving math & 中文 mini-pack

Three ten-minute activities for the week before Thanksgiving: multiply by 11 with turkeys, share pumpkin pies with double-and-half, and trace 谢, the character for thanks. Do them here with instant checking, or print the free 3-page PDF.

🦃Turkey times 11

Each turkey has a two-digit number on its feathers. Multiply it by 11 in your head, type the answer, and tap Check.

The trick: split the two digits apart, add them, and put the sum in the middle. 23 × 11 → 2, (2+3), 3 → 253. If the middle sum is 10 or more, carry the 1 to the left.

🥧Pumpkin pie halves

Each pie problem is "something × 5". Halve the even number and double the 5, and the hard problem turns into an easy one.

The trick: 14 × 5 → half of 14 is 7, double 5 is 10 → 7 × 10 = 70. The answer stays the same because the 2 just moves across.
