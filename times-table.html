Times Table Explorer

A multiplication chart you can tap. Touch any square to light up its row and column, read a tip for that times table, then hide some squares and find them again. 点一下格子，看 12 以内的乘法口诀和小窍门，再把答案藏起来考考自己。

Tap the chart

🔢Tap a square

The blue squares show how to count your way there. The yellow square is the flipped version of the same fact: 3 × 7 and 7 × 3 are twins. Tap a number on the edge to light up its whole table.

7 × 8 = 56

7 rows of 8 make 56.

Swipe the chart sideways to see all 12 columns.

Shortcuts for the hard rows.

Fold a finger for the nines, split the digits for elevens, or halve one number and double the other. Printable 10-page packs are $1.99 each on Etsy.

For grown-ups: say the fact out loud before tapping a hidden square, and start with the 2s, 5s and 10s before the 7s and 8s. Use "Print this chart" for a clean one-page version to stick on the fridge. Nothing on this page is saved or sent anywhere.
