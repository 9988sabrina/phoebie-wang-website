About Wild Case Files

Each case is a short, silly comic strip: listen to the owner, examine the animal, put the clues together, make a diagnosis and treat it in order. A wrong first guess is fine — you get a hint and look again, just like a real vet would. Afterwards, visit the Care Room to feed, bathe, dress up and tuck in your patients — pretend play with no wrong answers. The stories are simplified; a sick or injured real animal always needs a real vet.
