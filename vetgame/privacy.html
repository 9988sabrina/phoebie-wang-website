Wild Clinic Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version

Wild Clinic (also called Wild Case Files / 野生诊所) does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your game progress (cases solved, stamps, pet names, care-room items, sound and language choices) is saved only on your own device.
  • If you turn on the daily reminder in the iOS app, the reminder time is saved on your device and the notification is scheduled by iOS itself. Nothing is sent to us.

Deleting the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, removes all of it.

Children

The game can be played by children and adults. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Not veterinary advice

Wild Clinic is a game. If a real animal is unwell, please see a real vet.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through phoebiewang.com.

野生诊所隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日

野生诊所（Wild Clinic / Wild Case Files）不收集、不上传、不出售、不分享任何个人信息。没有账号，没有广告，没有统计分析，没有第三方追踪。

游戏进度（已完成的病例、印章、宠物名字、小屋物品、声音和语言设置）只保存在你自己的设备上。如果你在 iOS 版里打开每日提醒，提醒时间也只保存在设备上，由 iOS 系统发出通知，不会发送给我们。删除 App 或清除浏览器网站数据后，这些内容全部消失。

孩子和大人都可以玩。因为游戏不收集任何人的信息，所以也不收集儿童的信息。

野生诊所是游戏，不是兽医建议。真实的动物生病了，请找真正的兽医。

政策如有变化，会在本页更新并标注新日期。有问题可以通过 phoebiewang.com 联系。
